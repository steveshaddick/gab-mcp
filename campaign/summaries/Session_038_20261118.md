# Session 38 — The Hag's Red Bracelets

**Date played:** 2026-11-18  
**Players present:** Fic Torastro (Jake), Tordrug Slaatewalker (Phil), PQNine Ten (Steve), Hairy Styles (sameaslasttime), Baron Halfhammer (Jeff)  
**Source transcript:** `transcripts/Session_038_20261118.docx`  
**Status:** draft

## What happened
- DM recap: Idrissa / Constantori payout, the Shard of Xeluan arc, Hairy's night with Mabel, plan to head north up the Long Road through Red Larch and Amphail to the hamlet of **Raslantar** by the **Hallowbrand Family Chapel** just outside Waterdeep. ⚠️ DM consistently said "Halibrand" / "Zeluin" / "Kael Vor, the Veiled Maul" / "Mabel Axels Thorne" — drift back from Steve's PR #11 canonical spellings; see the ingest log and open threads.
- **DM confirms Mabel's daughter is named Halda** — in the recap line "she had a daughter named Halda." **Mabel "Axles" Thorne is Sister Halda Thorne's mother.** Baron's Session-37 hunch pays off.
- Party debates rushing vs. normal pace; decides to travel normally with the cart (three days expected) since they don't actually know Lord Hallowbrand's expedition head-start.
- **Hairy stops by Mabel on the way out of Westbridge.** She gifts the 6" granite cube **free** — says she considers him a friend and offers to **name-drop him in Amphail** to vouch for him. Hairy stashes it in the Bag of Holding, still can't remember what he wanted it for.
- PQNine retrieves his "invisible feathered hat" from Baron.
- **Road encounter — the funeral rehearsal.** An elderly dying matron lies in an open coffin while six peasant pallbearers practice looking sad. She wants outside opinion on whether her son looks grieved enough. PQNine insight-checks the son — "face sad, eyes not." PQNine tells her the son can't truly mourn while she's still alive. She smacks the son with her funeral flowers and the procession moves on.
- **Road encounter — the stranded troll cage.** A prisoner-transport cage with no horse (the chained troll dragged the AI-drawn six-legged horse into the woods) and two panicked guards on the roof. Guards offer **20 gp to re-cage, 30 gp to kill**. Tordrug's history (**26**) dumps full troll lore on the party: strong, fast, keen smell, speaks Giant only, regenerates unless hit with **fire or acid**.
- **Troll fight:** Tordrug Fire Bolt opens; Baron sticks Hunter's Mark arrows; PQNine fails a grapple; **Hairy Flame Blade** (first use); **Fic finishes with a sneak-attack rapier strike** (20 to hit, 17 damage). Fire damage from Flame Blade + Fire Bolt prevents regen — Tordrug confirms it's permanently dead. **Guards pay 30 gp (6 gp each).** Party leaves the dead troll with the guards and moves on. ⚠️ Who was paying to transport a live troll, and where to, is unanswered.
- **Red Larch at supper.** Dusty quarry village ~600, built on a spring at a crossroads. Tordrug recognizes it from his own thesis on "magic in Red Larch" (nat-20 history). Known places: **The Swinging Sword** inn (5 sp/person incl. stabling), **Helver Tarnall's** road-supplies shop, and **Maliko Stoneworks** — the big stoneworks, likely Mabel's old employer and the namesake-root of Tordrug's Slaatewalker surname. Audible quarry hammers before town is visible.
- **At the Swinging Sword:** Baron pays 2 gp 5 sp for all five rooms. Fic buys roast chicken + beef, pays an extra silver to have it grilled wrap-style ("breakfast burrito"). PQNine drinks dwarven whiskey from his pewter mug and strikes up a conversation with **a Red Larch rock gnome tinkerer** — runs a conveyor-belt "wheelie bobber" at the quarry that tripled productivity. PQNine shows him Elix's dented steam regulator; the gnome admires the workmanship, flags it as **foreign-sourced**, says gears like that need to come **from Waterdeep**. He also compliments PQNine's invisible hat ("virile"). They part as friends.
- Fic asks around about horse trainers; locals point him to **Amphail** (DM spells it out: "AM-P-H-A-I-L") as **THE** horse-breeding town supplying the Waterdeep country estates. Hairy and Tordrug eat and head to bed early (Tordrug only water). Full long rest.
- **Next day on the road — the Lucrotas.** Fic (lead on horse) spots a riderless mule frozen in the road staring into the bushes; a man's voice calls "Over here, my leg!" from the brush. Fic smells a trap; Eye Stone doesn't glow. Initiative.
- **Two Lucrotas** — shaggy amalgams (badger head, hyena body, bear forelegs, horse hindquarters with cloven hooves; rotting-meat breath). **They mimic the human voice through unmoving mouths.** PQNine recognizes them from childhood stories as **voice-mimicking bait predators** (the mule's owner is presumably already in their stomachs). Hairy Hastes PQNine. The Lucrotas bite/kick PQNine (one crit) and later Baron; one kills the mule ("Barney") on an opportunity attack. Hairy's 3rd-level Magic Missile finishes the first. Baron crits an arrow (**25 damage**) into the second's eye; Fic finishes it with an acrobatic rapier strike to the skull. Tordrug burns both corpses on a pyre (they stink worse burning).
- **Road encounter — the sweaty wagon driver.** Twitchy roadside man pretending to "repair" a broken wheel but miming it, obsessively guarding a small lidded tin he keeps staring at. Asks if the party are King's Men ("I heard you have to say"); rambles about his mother's kitchen (she's been dead 20 years); waves off help. Fic only rolls 7 on perception but notes the mime job. Party leaves him alone. ⚠️ What's in the tin?
- **Amphail outskirts — the scarecrow farmer.** Open paddocks and hayfields. A farmer calls for help from atop a stone wall bordering a hayfield full of **animated scarecrows** and one burn mark where he threw his lantern. He asks the party to grab a **red braided-string bracelet** off the gate and bring it to him — the scarecrows won't attack anyone wearing one. Initiative.
- **Scarecrow fight:** before the party can act, a scarecrow KOs the farmer on the wall — **all scarecrows instantly freeze**. When PQNine (spider-climbing the wall) re-enters the field, they reanimate. **Scarecrow hits force a Wisdom save — Fic fails and becomes Frightened**, climbs down and flees 30 ft. Tordrug puts on a bracelet and the scarecrows ignore him. Tordrug uses **Mage Hand to pluck the red bracelet off a scarecrow** and that scarecrow instantly drops inert; he hands one to PQNine (DM ruled he can't both remove and hand off in the same turn). Baron crosses the wall and casts **Cure Wounds (lvl 1, 5 HP)** on the farmer, waking him. Hairy repeatedly Mage-Hand plucks bracelets (DM requires a d6 per attempt; a 2 jams him). Tordrug casts **Fireball** on a scarecrow — it keeps swinging while burning; Fic recovers from frightened and finishes the burning scarecrow with his rapier to the eye. Farmer begs them not to destroy the rest.
- Farmer explains: he once fed a passing **hag**; she repaid him by animating his scarecrows to attack thieves, **keyed to the red bracelets**. He had forgotten his on the gate when he entered the field. All he has on him is 4 cp — offers a cooked meal and shelter instead.
- Party accepts. Farmer takes them home, introduces his wife and three kids, serves roast chicken + salt pork + saved-up wine. Great spread. **DM awards inspiration to each PC.** Party sleeps in the hayloft (declines the kids' beds — Hairy noted he'd destroy one). Session ends.
