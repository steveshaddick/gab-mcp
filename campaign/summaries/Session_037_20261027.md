## What happened
- Morning at the forest camp. Party confirms PQNine is attuned to **Constantori**, who greets them with sardonic insults and short-list jabs at Hairy's hygiene and Baron's woodcraft.
- Hairy floats going back to loot Elix's workshop (the 2× 1,000 gp spyglasses, the coffin priest with the gold sun death mask, the straw crate) and even impersonating Elix to take over the Agile Hand guild. Party decides it's not worth it right now and shelves the idea.
- Confirm they already pulled the Agile Hand ledger from the quartermaster office and will deliver it to Idrissa alongside the portrait.
- **Fic presses Constantori on Lord Hallebrand.** Constantori names **Lady Meredith Hallowbrand** — Hallebrand's grandmother — as his favorite former owner; she kept him in her study, read aloud to him, and asked his opinion. Her grandson wanted the portrait, but it was stolen before he could take it. ⚠️ Lady Meredith is rendered as "Hallowbrand"; family name spelling still unsettled (see glossary + ingest log).
- Constantori volunteers lore about **the Shard of Zeluin**: abyssal pendant that inflicts misery on its wielders and their enemies to feed a long-forgotten demon, **Kale Vore, the Veiled Maw** (DM typed the name in Teams chat). **Lord Hallebrand is financing an expedition to retrieve it.**
- Constantori adds: the shard lies in **Lady Meredith's crypt beneath the Hallebrand Family Chapel near Waterdeep.** She wanted to be buried **wearing it as a pendant** specifically so her grandson Hallebrand could never have it. PQNine rolls History with advantage — doesn't recognize the chapel's exact location.
- PQNine straps the sentient, eye-tracking, magic-missile-capable portrait to his back like a backpack. Hairy threatens to pee on it; PQNine scuttles away; they stash it properly for the walk.
- On the road near the Elix ambush site they spot **King's men and the rumpled trench-coat "Columbo" investigator** (likely Investigator Tam Vell) working the spot where Elix was buried. Party is ignored and keeps walking.
- Stop at the **Tipsy Tankard**: PQNine buys a **replacement pewter ale mug for 1 gp** (to replace the one buried with Elix).
- **At Idrissa Caramorte's barn:** party hands over Constantori's Portrait and the Agile Hand ledger. **Idrissa pays 2,000 gp for the portrait + a 500 gp bonus for the ledger** (turns out to be an inventory of stolen goods and fence prices — "above and beyond"). **Party take: 2,500 gp (500 gp each).**
- PQNine formally de-attunes from Constantori and says goodbye; the painting goes quiet.
- Fic pitches Idrissa on recognizing them if they ever take over the Agile Hand. She says she'd happily do business again and hints she may have another job soon — will send a courier. Fic fishes for info on "Kale Vore of the Veldma" framed as art; she hasn't heard of it. She doesn't know Hallebrand ("I don't follow politics").
- Travel back to the Harvest Inn. Fic haggles with **Haravin Dar Dragon** over *A Proper Elven Courtship*. Haravin tries to bump the price to 90 gp claiming another buyer. **Hairy's insight + persuasion (nat 25)** talks him back to the original 60 gp + 2 gp handler's fee. **Painting bought for 62 gp**, stored on consignment at the Harvest Inn until Fic claims it.
- Over Dar Dragon Deluxe burritos and lagers the party talks about **buying a base in Waterdeep** — they remember they burned part of the city down "like a day and a half ago" (in-fiction) and speculate that the **Delphi Mansion** / old-church quarter / burnt South Ward might be cheap fixer-uppers now.
- Hairy spends the evening table-to-table **challenging patrons to arm-wrestle**, backhandedly insulting ("nice hat") anyone who refuses — his explanation to PQNine of "hobnobbing with bigwigs" (saying one thing to mean another) becomes a running bit.
- A wiry old woman accepts. Hairy wins (**Athletics 19**) by a narrower margin than expected and buys her five beers. They get to talking.
- **Mabel Axelthorn** — retired-but-still-active human quarry teamster who hauled stone out of **Red Larch** for 40 years. Her kids died of plague, her husband in a quarry accident, and her one surviving daughter **joined the Order of the Vale / Sisters of the Vale** — a loss Mabel treats as a death, calling the order "cultist fanatics."
- Hairy asks about Hallebrand. Mabel calls him "nasty," shakes her head whenever his name comes up, and refuses to discuss politics further.
- Hairy commissions a **6"×6"×6" block of granite** from her for the next morning for **8 cp**; privately decides to pay her **8 gp** (a bag of coins) instead.
- Baron (crudely) points out that befriending Mabel sets up a future needle against Sister Halda if she turns out to be Mabel's daughter — the group banks the connection ⚠️.
- Session ends with the party settling into the Harvest Inn's six-bed room, planning to head up the Long Road through **Red Larch** and **Amphail** toward Waterdeep next session, still debating Waterdeep property and whether they'll be recognized from the fire.

Night at the Harvest Inn in Westbridge. Constantori and the Agile Hand ledger are delivered; 2,500 gp split (500 each). Fic has *A Proper Elven Courtship* on consignment with Haravin, PQNine is de-attuned from the painting, and Hairy is drunk upstairs waiting for Mabel to deliver his granite block at sunrise +2.
