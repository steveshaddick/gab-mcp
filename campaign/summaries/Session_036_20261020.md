## What happened
- DM recap of S35 and the party rolls initiative, picking up mid-break-in with the portrait screaming. Persistent VTT confusion as tokens disappear when the DM moves between Dusk's bedroom and the Tower Peak above it.
- **Tordrug (still disguised as Grinky)** opens the quartermaster-office door and tells the knocking guard "Boss says keep everybody out" — Persuasion 13 passes, guard stays put. Tordrug then crosses to Dusk's bedroom and asks "What's going on, boss?" to cover.
- **Invisible Baron** ransacks Dusk's writing desk in the Tower Peak and finds **sketches and descriptions of mechanical traps**. He decides they're not worth taking (foreshadowing).
- **Dusk** dashes upstairs toward the Tower Peak alcove. **Her lover** (**played as male this session** — scantily-clad noble, see ⚠️ on "Dawn" below) rises half-dressed from the bed and calls "Elix, what are you doing here?" at Hairy.
- Fic hides behind a statue in the Tower Peak under his **Cloak of Elvenkind** and readies a crossbow bolt. **Invisible PQNine** readies a trip for anyone running up the stairs.
- **Hairy pulls Constantori's Portrait off its hooks and triggers a hidden poison-gas trap behind it.** DC 14 CON save, 33 damage on fail / 16 on save. **Baron fails (33)**, **Hairy half (16)**, **Fic saves**. Dusk out of range on the stairs.
- **Hairy stuffs the portrait into the party's password-locked Bag of Holding** (one action), then **Quickens Chill Touch** (2 sorcery points) on Dusk for 16 necrotic, muttering incoherently to keep her confused.
- Downstairs the noble lover ignores the chaos, climbs back into bed, lights his pipe and blows smoke rings. **Tordrug casts Charm Person on him**; he shrugs and keeps smoking — effectively uninterested either way ⚠️ (saved or just doesn't care).
- Baron drinks a healing potion while staying invisible and readies a bow shot. Fic drinks a 2d4+2 potion (+12 HP) and also readies a bolt.
- PQNine sneaks up the stairs, slips through the half-square beside Dusk on a DEX check, Stealths with advantage to stay invisible on arrival.
- **Dusk reaches Hairy and attacks with her two short swords** — misses once, **crits (23) the second time**, and **drops Hairy unconscious (21 damage)**.
- Baron breaks invisibility to shoot — 19 to hit for 11 damage on Dusk, Hunter's Mark queued. Fic, still hidden under the Cloak, lands a **sneak-attack rapier for 11** (second rapier swing retconned — he'd already used his bonus action on the potion).
- Hairy makes a death save. PQNine fails his Perception-with-disadvantage to spot Fic and ends up just "loving life" invisible beside the party.
- **Tordrug runs upstairs performatively yelling "Enix! What happened to Enix?!"** and pours a healing potion down the unconscious Hairy's throat. DM rules Hairy's Disguise Self as Elix stays up — not a concentration spell.
- Baron slaps Hunter's Mark on Dusk and fires two arrows; **the second drops Guildmaster Dusk**. Hairy stands up, kicks her corpse, and loots her **two short swords** (she's otherwise in her underwear).
- Hairy stashes Dusk's body under the writing desk, then **casts Disguise Self on Fic to look like Elix** so Fic can walk out with "Grinky" and "Elix" for the group's planned exit. Hairy keeps his own Elix disguise running.
- Follow-up investigation of Dusk's desk goes nowhere — Baron and PQNine are still woozy and streaming-eyed from the lingering poison and find nothing valuable beyond more trap schematics.
- **Exit:** Hairy pulls one of the red Agile Hand cloaks out of the Bag of Holding to cover his scant Elix disguise, bluffs the hallway guard with "Keep watching, don't let anybody in," and leaves. On the way down they notice **the noble lover is gone from the bedroom** — he escaped.
- Party walks out into the woods around midnight. Baron uses **Natural Explorer (forest)** to cover tracks. Short rest: Hairy burns 2 hit dice, Tordrug uses **Arcane Recovery**.
- **Long rest in the forest.** Baron takes first watch (uneventful; DM's mic battery dies mid-watch, replaced).
- **PQNine takes second watch, lures Tordrug off to "fan out,"** then **sneaks the portrait out of the Bag of Holding** (he knows the password). Stealths past Hairy; Tordrug catches him and just doesn't care.
- **The portrait wakes up and introduces himself as Constantori**, "the most beautiful man in the world," ~683 years old. **After being insulted into it, PQNine attunes to Constantori as a magic item.**
- **Constantori's attunement perks:**
  - **True sight.**
  - **Telepathic link to PQNine** (one-way: PQNine thinks, Constantori answers).
  - Can be told to **"guard" a hallway or room**.
- Under questioning Constantori spills a bunch of lore:
  - **Idrissa Caramorte's orders are to recover him "without being questioned,"** but she personally wants to question him first — she believes he **witnessed someone's disappearance** ⚠️.
  - Idrissa once **commissioned a separate screaming portrait** rumored to **contain a man's soul**.
  - **Idrissa is secretly funding an orphanage with Cognoscenti / guild money** ⚠️.
  - Hidden Agile Hand loot still in **Elix's office** (the receiving/goods room G12): a wooden crate with **2× spyglasses worth 1,000 gp each** packed in straw, and **a coffin with a dirty priest wearing a holy symbol and a gold sun-shaped death mask**.
  - **Elix the Saint got his nickname** because he was the only Agile Hand thief who **didn't skim from the guild**.
- Morning. Party wakes to find PQNine chatting with the portrait. Constantori proceeds to insult Fic ("a rat that learned to use tools"), Hairy ("an owl bear coughed you up" — he could smell him through the Elix disguise from downstairs during the fight), Tordrug ("a bookmark with delusions of grandeur," later walked back after a Minor Illusion duplicate), and the group generally. **Hairy and Fic openly threaten to burn the painting; PQNine insists on keeping it.**
- Mid-conversation **Fic pivots and asks Constantori about Duke / Lord Hallebrand** (heard this session as "Hallibrand" — DM paused to look up his own notes before confirming the spelling ⚠️). **Constantori confirms he was in Hallebrand's presence "when he was a young soldier."**
