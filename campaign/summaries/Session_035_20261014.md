# Session 35 — The Tipsy Tankard Chase

**Date played:** 2026-10-14  
**Players present:** Fic Torastro (Jake), Tordrug Slaatewalker (Phil), PQNine Ten (Steve), Hairy Styles (sameaslasttime), Baron Halfhammer (Jeff)

> ⚠️ Candidate alt-title: **"The Screaming Portrait"** — the Agile Hand HQ break-in in the second half is a bigger beat than the tavern chase; keep or rename at the table.

## What happened
- DM recap bridges from S34: party rode out of Idrissa's barn, aimed for the Tipsy Tankard, planning to ambush Elix the Saint for his master key to the Agile Hand HQ.
- **The Tipsy Tankard** — a "medieval truck stop" tavern in a hamlet about 45 min walk from the Agile Hand HQ. Big hearth, two bartenders (older stout one, younger one), wagon drivers out front. Hairy and Baron stay outside with the cart to watch for a runner; Fic, Tordrug, and PQNine go in (Tordrug ditches his Get Around Boys jacket).
- PQNine fixates on the tavern's unusually well-cast pewter mugs stamped with a hat-and-apple design. (Buys one on his way out for 1 gp.)
- Fic buys rounds for the whole bar (24 patrons, ~6 sp per round, extra silver for the keep) to loosen the room up and pours his own beer out to stay sober; sings a bawdy drinking song that catches on.
- Tordrug spots Elix: alone in the gangster seat under the stairs, picking onions out of his supper, eyes the door every time it opens, and the younger of the two bartenders genuinely lights up when serving him.
- Tordrug pays Elix's 1 gp tab to the younger bartender to confirm the name ("Elix? Yeah, that's him") and stands to leave. When the bartender relays the paid tab, **Elix clocks the party, nods, excuses himself to the privy, and bolts out the back at a full sprint toward the woods.** Fic hoods up and chases.
- **Combat on the dark country road.** Elix trips on a rock in the dark (nat 1) and lands prone. Baron shoots at range for 18 damage (then holds a Horde Breaker shot). PQNine grapples/pins Elix. Elix flails with scimitar + dagger, slicing attempted rope-tie-ups and hitting PQNine (12, then 6). Tordrug and Hairy both hit with **Shocking Grasp** ("Homeland Security taser gloves"). Fic lands a rapier sneak attack (3d6 sneak dice, "Rapier Sploon") and chooses to **knock Elix unconscious** rather than kill. Party argues briefly about killing him; agree to try charming him first.
- Search and loot Elix: **a heavy brass key with three square teeth on a leather cord** (guild-house master key), coin purse 18 gp / 24 sp / a cheap brass wedding ring, two daggers, a scimitar, and a worn wooden Saint figurine. **Loot split: 4 gp 1 sp per party member.** Fic takes the master key (worn on neck) and one dagger. Tordrug takes the other dagger. Hairy pockets the brass wedding ring and the saint figurine. Scimitar discarded.
- Tordrug casts **Charm Person** on the unconscious Elix. Elix fails the DC 16 Wis save. Tordrug wakes him posing as a rescuer — "there was a huge fight, we dragged the bodies away." Elix buys it from Tordrug but is suspicious of Fic.
- **Interrogation:** Elix confirms the brass key is the guild-house master key. **Insists there is no vault.** The guild doesn't normally keep loot — they fence it, and Dusk handles the fence. **Guildmaster Dusk's office** is where the best stuff lives. Doesn't know which room Constantori's Portrait is in. The basement has a training/traps course that gets rearranged often; **there is no secret door INTO the basement**, only one connecting the two basements. ⚠️ (Note: this contradicts Grinky's "rumored outside secret door" claim.)
- Charm Person wears off. **Tordrug kills Elix with Shocking Grasp.**
- **Burial scene (hauled further down the road in the cart):** PQNine says a prayer and buries Elix with the pewter mug he just bought at the Tankard. Tordrug marks the grave with a 30-second Minor Illusion crucifix. Hairy keeps the wooden saint figurine; also keeps Elix's cheap brass wedding ring.
- **Infiltration plan:** Hairy disguises as Elix (taller than him, but close enough); Tordrug disguises as Grinky (loosely tied as a prisoner being brought back). Tordrug casts **Invisibility (3rd lvl)** on PQNine and Baron. Baron casts **Pass Without Trace** on the party (+10 Stealth, 1 hour). Fic uses his attuned **Cloak of Elvenkind** (hood up: advantage on Stealth, disadvantage on perception against him). PQNine lends Baron his "invisible hat." PQNine takes a short rest and drinks a healing potion (2d4+2 = 7 HP).
- **Approach to the Agile Hand HQ.** A balcony lookout hails "Elix, is that you?" Hairy-as-Elix answers in character, says he's bringing captured Grinky to the boss. The brass key opens the front door into the front office (map area **G10**).
- **Front office:** coin box behind the counter holds 15 cp + 11 sp — confirming Elix wasn't lying about the HQ being cash-light. Hairy takes it (divvy 2 sp each; Hairy keeps the coppers). **Three key hooks on the east wall; party takes the two keys, third hook empty.**
- Cross the main hall. Upstairs find a receiving/goods room (map **G12**) with wood shavings on the floor, lantern block-and-tackle, casks/crates, and **an earth-encrusted coffin with its lid ajar**. Fic trap-checks it (nothing); party decides to skip and press on.
- **Shared bedroom:** Hairy quietly opens the door onto two sleeping men. Closes it. Group Stealth checks saved by Pass Without Trace.
- **Quartermaster-style office:** Hairy knocks accidentally, a confused guard answers ("why are you knocking on your own office?"). Hairy covers — "trouble at the Tankard, place shut early" — passes a Persuasion with advantage. Fails to persuade the guard to leave his post; then **Intimidates (18)** him into standing down outside and shuts the door. Takes the desk's **leather-bound ledger**, quill, ink. Draws the curtains to block the balcony lookout's sightline.
- **Guildmaster's round bedroom:** Hairy quietly opens the door. Canopied bed with **Guildmaster Dusk (female, confirmed) and her lover** (sleeping together, half-undressed; Dusk murmurs "Goodnight, Dawn. Love you." ⚠️ Dawn may be a pet name). Mantle: soap, brush, clay pipe, ivory skull-shaped tobacco jar. Leather trunk at foot of bed; PQNine opens it — linens over a crossbow, nothing else. **Two staircases hug the walls: one goes down (back to first floor), one goes up — the up-staircase was NOT on Grinky's map.**
- **Fic and Baron ascend to the Tower Peak:** pewter chandelier, conical roof, cluttered writing desk, padded chair, and in a shallow alcove near the stairhead — a gold-framed **portrait of a beautiful, well-dressed man.** Its eyes track them.
- **The portrait speaks: "I am. What are you doing here?"** Fic (not visible but audible) replies "Admiring a great work of art." The portrait is not flattered — **it starts screaming "Help, help, help!"** alerting the HQ.
- Guildmaster Dusk and her lover wake; she calls out "Helix, what's going on?" Hairy (still disguised) deflects and runs for the up-stairs intending to shove the portrait into a bag of holding to shut it up. A guard knocks at the window and another at the door of the round bedroom. **DM calls initiative;** everyone rolls; DM realizes he hasn't statted the portrait yet, so **pauses the session there**.
