# Session 35 — The Tipsy Tankard Chase

**Date played:** 2026-10-14  
**Players present:** Fic Torastro (Jake), Tordrug Slaatewalker (Phil), PQNine Ten (Steve), Hairy Styles (sameaslasttime), Baron Halfhammer (Jeff)  
**Source transcript:** `transcripts/Session_035_20261014.docx`  
**Status:** draft

## What happened
- DM recap bridges from S34: party met collector Haravin Dar Dragon, were introduced to Idrissa Caramorte, interrogated captured thief Grink "Grinky" Brithwort (Tordrug charmed him), and learned to target **Elix the Saint** at the **Tipsy Tankard** for his master key.
- Party reaches the **Tipsy Tankard** — a "medieval truck stop" tavern in a small hamlet about 45 min walk from the Agile Hand guild house. Hairy and Baron stay outside with the cart to watch for a runner; Fic, Tordrug, and PQNine go in (Tordrug ditches his Get Around Boys jacket).
- PQNine fixates on the tavern's unusually well-cast pewter mugs stamped with a hat-and-apple design; gets no useful info out of it (later buys one for 1 gp when he leaves).
- Fic buys rounds for the whole bar (24 patrons, ~6 sp per round, extra silver for the keep) to loosen the room up, pouring his own beer out to stay sober.
- Tordrug spots Elix: alone in the gangster seat under the stairs, picking onions out of his supper, eyes the door every time it opens, and the younger of the two bartenders genuinely lights up when serving him.
- Tordrug pays Elix's 1 gp tab to the younger bartender to confirm the name ("Elix? Yeah, that's him"), then stands to leave. When the bartender relays the paid tab, Elix clocks the party, nods, excuses himself to the privy, and bolts out the back at a full sprint toward the woods.
- Fic yells "Gabby's bolted," signals outside, and gives chase. Whole party pursues on a dark country road. Elix trips on a rock in the dark and lands prone (DM: fortunate failure).
- **Combat.** Baron shoots at range for 18 damage (then holds a Horde Breaker shot). PQNine grapples/pins Elix. Fic tries to tie him up; Elix slices the rope with his scimitar. Tordrug moves in with **Shocking Grasp**. Hairy runs up and also hits with **Shocking Grasp** ("Homeland Security taser gloves"). Elix flails with scimitar and dagger — ~12 then ~11 damage to PQNine over two turns. Fic lands a rapier sneak attack (3d6 sneak dice, "Rapier Sploon") and chooses to knock Elix unconscious rather than kill. Party argues briefly about killing him; agree to try charming him first.
- Search and loot Elix: **a heavy brass key with three square teeth on a leather cord** (master key to the Agile Hand HQ), coin purse of 18 gp / 24 sp / a cheap brass wedding ring, two daggers, a scimitar, and a worn wooden Saint figurine. **Loot split: 4 gp 1 sp per party member** (five shares). Fic takes the master key (worn on neck) and one dagger. Tordrug takes the other dagger. The scimitar and figurine go unclaimed (PQNine suggests dumping the scimitar; Tordrug jokes about staging a seppuku).
- Tordrug casts **Charm Person**; Elix fails the DC 16 Wis save. Tordrug wakes him posing as a rescuer — "there was a huge fight, we dragged the bodies away." Elix buys it from Tordrug but is suspicious of Fic.
- **Interrogation:** Elix confirms the brass key is the guild-house master key; doesn't know which room Constantori's Portrait is in — "Guildmaster Dusk deals with that"; the guild doesn't normally keep loot, they fence it, and Dusk handles the fence too. Baron asks about a vault — session cuts off mid-answer.
