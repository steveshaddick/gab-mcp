# Session 34 — The Charmed Thief

## What happened
- Party is rested in Tribor planning to ride south to Westbridge to meet someone named Haravin; players bicker about whether they leveled last session and reminisce about blowing up a piano and Hairy shoving an old woman into the water in S33.
- Mission re-grounded: Sister Halda Thorne's sealed orders had her pose as the Get Around Boys to meet **Idrissa Caramorte** (Cognoscenti Esoterica), silence her, and recover **Constantori's portrait**. Westbridge is the next stop south on the Long Road.
- **Tribor Travelers** (caravan company yard): PQNine and Hairy try to hire a cargo run to Westbridge and send some of PQNine's money toward Waterdeep. Hairy drops **5 gp** on the clerk's board as a "bribe" to get the Get Around Boys on his list; the clerk pockets it, denies the party is on any list, and names **Erlam Stock Spool** as the actual contact.
- Hairy demands the money back; the clerk calls for the bailiff. About a dozen caravan guards close in with spears. PQNine is grappled, drops the grappler with a flurry of nonlethal unarmed strikes, Drunken-Technique-disengages. Hairy Misty Steps past the guards, Chill Touches the clerk at ~90 ft for 6 necrotic (unconscious), then Messages the party to flee.
- Baron (asleep in the cart at the Talking Troll stable) and the elves at the Northern Shield House hightail it out. The party clears Tribor ahead of the bailiff's posse and rides south at full speed. **The party is now wanted (or at least unwelcome) in Tribor.**
- Arrive at **Westbridge**: small muddy farming village on the Long Road between Tribor and Red Larch; smith, wheelwright, provision shop, livestock yard, shrine to Chauntea; under 100 villagers plus outlying farms.
- **The Harvest Inn** (west side of the road, sheaf-of-wheat sign): meet **Haravin Dar Dragon**, halfling innkeeper and self-styled "Dealer in Fine Arts." Jake buys the table Dar Dragon Deluxe burritos at 2 sp each and tips 1 gp for drinks.
- Haravin confirms Idrissa is a mutual contact, says *she* told him about the Get Around Boys, and offers to send them to her tomorrow. He ushers the party through a purple curtain to his back-room collection of five bawdy paintings (*The Miller's Wife* 40 gp, *The Ambassador's Visit* 150 gp, *The Halflings' Second Breakfast* 35 gp, *The Harvest* 250 gp with Haravin himself in it, *A Proper Elven Courtship* 60 gp). Fic eyes the courtship painting; nothing bought. PQNine tries to sell Haravin his Carl-of-Tribor handbill portrait; Haravin passes.
- Tordrug pays 3 gp (30 sp) for a shared six-bed room plus the empty sixth bed so no one else joins them. Haravin stables the horse and cart free and includes breakfast "because mutual friends of Idrissa." Tordrug blows an insight check on Haravin's unsettling interest in watching people eat his hot-sauce burritos and comes away rating Haravin very highly.
- **Breakfast:** at breakfast Haravin notices the **Sisters of the Vale** symbol around Hairy's neck and visibly flinches. Hairy claims he wears it as a "don't be like them" reminder. Haravin warns the Sisters are not welcome in most communities and would be very dangerous for Idrissa to deal with.
- Haravin's directions to Idrissa: ride halfway down the road south toward the next town, take a dirt road left (east), find a giant 300–400-year-old oak, follow the trail to a barn — "A drizzle will be there."
- Walking out of town PQNine spots a leopard-print-rag-wrapped beggar and drops **10 gp** on him; the beggar blesses him with Chauntea.
- **Idrissa's barn** (abandoned farm, dead cornfield, light rain): **Idrissa Caramorte** steps out — well-dressed human woman dressed like a mage/sorceress/wizard. Agent of the **Cognoscenti Esoterica**. She sent for the party via her courier **Jimmy Two Pouch**, a gnome who "pops in and out of existence"; the party tells her the Sisters of the Vale intercepted the message. She concedes Jimmy may have been coerced, not disloyal.
- Idrissa knows the Get Around Boys by reputation — the Meyerstone incident, the Afterlife Casino, the Delphi mansion, "a certain entity that tried to enter into our plane of existence." She offers **2,000 gp** for the safe return of **Constantori's Portrait**, stolen from a Cognoscenti vault by the **Agile Hand** thieves' guild (three Cognoscenti guards killed in the burglary).
- Up in the hayloft Idrissa shows them a captured Agile Hand thief she wants interrogated — scrawny, patchy-bearded, sweating, gagged, tied to a chair. Caught during the theft when his crew shoved him into the pursuing guards. Fic positions in the doorway with his rapier for the whole interrogation.
- Tordrug casts **Charm Person** (DC 16 Wis). Thief fails. Tordrug ungags him, poses as a fellow thief, flatters him, promises a cut of the painting, and gets him to draw up two inked maps: a route to the Agile Hand headquarters, and a floor-plan of it. Name: **Grink "Grinky" Brithwort** — 10 years with the guild, still bottom of the ladder.
- **Agile Hand HQ** per Grinky: ~1 day's ride (8–12 hr) from the barn. Four stories; main floor plus east and west buildings plus a tower. East building 2–3 floors. Guildmaster **Dusk**'s quarters are in the tower (2nd floor). Painting is probably there, OR in the basement. The basement is also recruit training and stores, with a **secret outside door** Grinky's never been through. Windows boarded, doors usually locked. Master-key holders: **Guildmaster Dusk**, **Elix the Saint** (lieutenant), **Jaymont the Sinner** (spy), and possibly **Wish**.
- The Agile Hand already has a buyer lined up for the painting — real time pressure.
- Best time to break in: late evening. Elix walks nightly to the nearby hamlet's inn — **The Tipsy Tankard** — to flirt with the bartender and returns late. Grinky suggests ambushing Elix on the way back for his master key.
- **Constantori's Portrait dossier** (from Idrissa):
  - Painted by **Deski Kwan** ⚠️ (DM only confirmed the "Kwan" ending; spelling guessed).
  - Commissioned by the late **Dayani Gristhorne**, a grand dame of the criminal underworld, as a sentient eavesdropping tool to plant in a rival's parlor.
  - Sentient. Talks. Has likes and dislikes. Vain. Remembers everything said in its presence. Subservient to its master.
  - Dusk is an assassin with a side career in extortion and blackmail; the painting is natural for her to keep rather than fence, but a sale is still possible.
  - The lead-lined bags the party took off Sister Halda are too small for the painting. Fic jokes about wearing them as hoods against the painting's charm.
- Plan agreed: ride out of the barn now at midday, aim to be near the Tipsy Tankard around midnight; ambush Elix on his walk back, take his master key, break into the Agile Hand HQ that night. Grinky stays bound at the barn under Idrissa's guard; she will release him alive when the job is done.
