# Session 34 — The Charmed Thief

## What happened
- DM recap: rested in Tribor; two leads for the portrait job — **Haravin at the Harvest Inn in Westbridge** (from Carl / the Tribor locals in S33) and **Idrissa Caramorte** (from Halda's sealed orders in S32). Party agrees to ride for Westbridge.
- **Tribor Travelers (caravan yard):** PQNine wants to ship money to Waterdeep; Hairy wants the Get Around Boys on the clerk's list for paid cargo hauls. The always-smiling clerk says they're not on his list. Hairy slides **5 gp** onto the clerk's board as a "bribe"; the clerk pockets it as a "tip" and names **Erlam Stock Spool** as the person who could actually add them — but Erlam is already gone.
- Hairy demands his gold back and intimidates the clerk; PQNine rips up the clerk's new list. **About 12 caravan guards close in with spears.**
- **Brawl.** PQNine is grappled; drops the grappler with a flurry of nonlethal unarmed strikes and uses **Drunken Technique** (free Disengage + 10 ft) to escape. Hairy **Misty Steps** past the guards and casts **Chill Touch** on the clerk at ~90 ft (19 to hit, 6 necrotic) — clerk drops unconscious. Hairy sends **Message** to the rest of the party: "some freak humans attacked us for no reason, we gotta go."
- Fic, Tordrug, and Baron (sleeping in the cart with the horse at Northfield's stable) hightail it out. Party rolls d20s to outrun the bailiff forming an informal posse and clears Tribor at full speed southbound on the Long Road. **The party is now wanted / unwelcome in Tribor.**
- Travel to **Westbridge**. Tordrug History: smaller than Tribor, muddy roadside hamlet between Tribor and Red Larch; smith, wheelwright, provision shop, livestock yard, shrine to Chauntea; **the Harvest Inn is the heart of it.**
- Arrive mid-afternoon at the **Harvest Inn**: curly-haired halfling behind the bar — **Haravin Dar Dragon**, "innkeeper, dealer in fine arts." Walls hung with paintings "a little too expensive for a little village inn."
- Fic buys the table **Dar Dragon Deluxe** burritos at 2 sp each (spiced beef, beans, rice, sharp cheese, roast peppers, "enough hot sauce to make Haravin watch you eat it"), drops 1 gp for drinks. **Haravin pulls up a chair to watch Fic eat.**
- Haravin: inherited the inn from his father, does "art on the side," buys/sells rarer pieces. Baron asks about "Darissa" → confirms **Idrissa Caramorte** is a mutual friend. Haravin clocks the GAB jacket, realizes they're the Get Around Boys. Offers **free stabling and breakfast** as a courtesy because of Idrissa.
- Back-room tour (behind a purple curtain): five bawdy paintings — *The Miller's Wife* 40 gp, *The Ambassador's Visit* 150 gp, *The Halflings' Second Breakfast* 35 gp, *The Harvest* 250 gp (with Haravin himself in it, in his current robes), *A Proper Elven Courtship* 60 gp. Fic eyes the courtship painting; nothing bought.
- PQNine tries to sell Haravin his Carl-of-Tribor handbill portrait. Haravin declines politely ("I've never heard of Carl").
- Lodging: Tordrug pays **3 gp** for the whole shared six-bed room so no strangers bunk in. Haravin leaves chocolates on each pillow; Baron grabs the unused sixth bed's chocolate too.
- Tordrug Insight-checks Haravin's unsettling interest in watching people eat his hot-sauce burritos ("it's art"). Fails. Comes away rating Haravin one of the most engaging, insightful people he's ever met ⚠️.
- **Breakfast:** Dar Dragon Deluxe burritos again, included. Fic thumb-flicks a 1 gp tip; Haravin catches it. **Haravin notices the Sisters-of-the-Vale symbol around Hairy's neck and visibly flinches.** Hairy spins it as a "don't be like them" reminder. Haravin warns the Sisters are not welcome in most communities and would be very dangerous for Idrissa to deal with.
- Haravin's directions to Idrissa: ride halfway down the road south toward the next town, turn left on a dirt road, find a giant 300–400-year-old oak, follow the trail to a barn — "A drizzle will be there."
- Leaving Westbridge, PQNine spots a leper in rags and drops **10 gp** on him; the leper blesses him with Chauntea.
- Travel through a dead cornfield in a light rain to the barn.
- **Idrissa Caramorte** steps out — well-dressed human woman dressed like a mage/sorceress/wizard. Agent of the **Cognoscenti Esoterica**. She sent for the party via her gnome courier **Jimmy Two Pouch** ("one of those magic shoes," pops in and out of existence, "runs like the wind"); the party tells her the Sisters of the Vale intercepted her message. She concedes Jimmy may have been coerced, not disloyal.
- Idrissa knows the Get Around Boys by reputation — the Meyerstone incident, the Afterlife Casino, the Delphi mansion, "a certain entity that tried to enter into our plane of existence." She offers **2,000 gp** for the safe return of **Constantori's Portrait**, stolen from a Cognoscenti vault by the **Agile Hand** thieves' guild (three Cognoscenti guards killed in the burglary).
- Up in the hayloft she shows them a captured Agile Hand thief she wants interrogated — scrawny, patchy-bearded, sweating, gagged, tied to a chair. Fic positions in the only doorway with his rapier for the whole interrogation.
- Tordrug casts **Charm Person** (DC 16 Wis). Thief fails. Tordrug ungags him, poses as a fellow thief, flatters him ("just keep practicing — perseverance"), promises a cut of the painting, and gets him to draw up two inked maps on paper Tordrug supplies: a route to the Agile Hand HQ, and a floor-plan of it. Name: **Grink "Grinky" Brithwort** — 10 years with the guild, still bottom of the ladder. Instantly smitten; asks what "resume" means.
- **Agile Hand HQ per Grinky:** ~1 day's ride (8–12 hr) from the barn. Four stories; main floor plus east and west buildings plus a tower. **Guildmaster Dusk's quarters are in the tower (2nd floor).** Painting is probably there, OR in the basement. Basement is recruit training + stores, and there is a **secret outside door** Grinky's never been through. Windows boarded, doors usually locked. Master-key holders: **Guildmaster Dusk**, **Elix the Saint** (lieutenant), **Jaymont the Sinner** (spy, "really good at blending in"), and possibly **Wish**.
- **The Agile Hand already has a buyer lined up for the painting** — real time pressure.
- Best time to break in: late evening. **Elix** walks nightly to the nearby hamlet's inn — **The Tipsy Tankard** — to flirt with the bartender and returns late. Grinky suggests ambushing Elix on the way back for his master key.
- **Constantori's Portrait dossier** (from Idrissa):
  - Painted by **Deski Kwan** ⚠️ (DM joke-spells it "5-8-Q-6"; only the "Kwan" ending confirmed).
  - Commissioned by the late **Dayani Gristhorne**, grand dame of the criminal underworld, as a sentient eavesdropping tool to plant in a rival's parlor.
  - Sentient. Talks. Has likes and dislikes. Vain. Remembers everything said in its presence. Subservient to its master.
  - Dusk is an assassin with a side career in extortion and blackmail; natural to keep the painting rather than fence, but a sale is still possible.
  - The lead-lined bags from Sister Halda are too small for the painting. Fic jokes about wearing them as hoods against the painting's charm.
- Fic confirms on record he is NOT a card-carrying Agile Hand member — "heard of them," wants to "make a splash" with them someday. Baron's parting setup question: does the Agile Hand know Fic's face? (DM defers to pre-game bookkeeping.)
- Plan agreed: ride out now at midday, aim to be near the Tipsy Tankard around midnight; ambush Elix, take his master key, break into the Agile Hand HQ that night. Grinky stays bound at the barn under Idrissa's guard; she will release him alive when the job is done.
