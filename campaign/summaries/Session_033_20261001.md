# Session 33 — Backwater Shopping in Tribor

**Date played:** 2026-10-01  
**Players present:** Fic Torastro (Jake), Tordrug Slaatewalker (Phil), PQNine Ten (Steve), Hairy Styles (sameaslasttime), Baron Halfhammer (Jeff)  
**Source transcript:** `transcripts/Session_033_20261001.docx`  
**Status:** draft

## What happened
- DM delivers a long "Ted Sessions back" recap stretching from Rebel's End through Little Lockford, Smolderton, the Wishkey Gear, the Slonk/Uzik aftermath, and the second defeat of Sister Halda Thorne — including the orders found on her (Idrissa Caramorte, Constantori's portrait, the Westbridge map circled "Haravin?"). The table bickers over whether they actually "freed" Slonk and Uzik; consensus is they at least enabled the escape.
- Morning in camp outside Little Lockford: Fic cants Shadow Strider, PQNine finishes his last shot of whiskey, Hairy practices grappling-hook throws with his silk rope. Agree Lord Hallebrand (Hairy's target) comes *after* Shadow Strider is trained.
- Baron rolls Survival (16) to lead the party back to the river at Miller's Reach, where they wait for the barge.
- **Jordan Hale** returns at noon with his one-eyed cattle barge, now hauling cattle down to the Underdark. Hairy tips him 1 gp up front and 2 gp on arrival; Jordan takes a shine to "Harry the Bugbear."
- **River encounter — the bottle:** a ragged, near-toothless old peasant woman on a skiff flags them down to retrieve a corked bottle from the reeds. Tordrug Mage Hands it aboard and reads the note aloud: *"I know what you did."* She leaves it with them. Hairy Mage Hand-shoves her into the river out of spite. **Tordrug keeps the bottle and note.**
- **River encounter — the piano:** a half-submerged white baby grand drifts downstream, keys plinking on the rocks. A woman on one bank claims it; a man on the other says she pushed it in. Tordrug Mage-Hands a tune out of it; Hairy grapple-hooks it (athletics 16) but can't lift without tipping the barge, drops it; Fic Fire Bolts the piano and they drift on.
- **Tribor, mid-afternoon.**
- **Full Hammer's Forge:** Hairy tries to sell Sister Halda's full gear. **Full Hammer refuses the plate** (Sisters-of-the-Vale insignia on the breastplate — "zealots, don't want them knowing I had this") and warns repeatedly the order are terrifying. Buys the chainmail for **20 gp**, then at Hairy's request **forges the salvaged Sisters-of-the-Vale insignia into a chain necklace** for 10 gp (net 10 gp cash + necklace). Hairy keeps the plate and Halda's longsword. Hairy sells a plain shortsword for 4 gp. Tordrug sells a Lord's Alliance longsword (6 gp) and splint armor (95 gp). Hairy introduces himself to the smith as **"Perry."**
- Full Hammer tells Fic that Tribor is too small to train Shadow Strider — needs a bigger city.
- **The Lion's Share:** newly franchised by the **Lion Shield Coster** of Yartar (old rival of Tribor); co-owners **Alestra Algar** (not met) and **Narth Tezran** (friendly, upsells). Common adventuring gear plus four 50 gp healing potions. PQNine asks after potions of giant strength (none), "water from a river that flows backwards" (sold out), and the local post office; Narth points him to **Tribor Travelers**.
- **The upscale inn — Northfield / North Shield House:** party picks it over the Talking Troll, Tribor Arms, Six Windows, and Pleasing Ladder. Innkeeper **Courtney** is a retired adventurer who keeps three mastiffs in the common room; staff described as unusually loyal; a lone tiefling patron is reading quietly in the back. Rooms 8 sp to 2 gp, breakfast included, horse stabled. Baron will sleep in the cart with the horse.
- Dinner: pan-fried trout with onions and brown bread; Fic rebrands it a "breakfast burrito." PQNine buys 2 full bottles of local Tribor rye at 1 gp each to top up his flask (peppery, American-style).
- Tordrug recalls college-era book knowledge: **Constantori's Portrait is a masterpiece** depicting one of the most beautiful men who ever lived; rumored occult properties; keeps getting stolen before anyone can study it properly. Fic frames it as his "Mona Lisa."
- Fic buys the adjacent table of four a 16-cp round and a full silver meal to pump them for info — learns about **Carl** (town portrait artist / town drunk, haunts the Talking Troll) and **Haravin**, who owns the **Harvest Inn in Westbridge** and is "a dealer in fine art." Westbridge is confirmed as a day's cart south, arrival by sunset if they leave in the morning. **This resolves the "Haravin?" circled on Halda's map.**
- Hairy picks up his finished Sisters-of-the-Vale insignia chain necklace and immediately wears it in public, inventing a cover story about having "a sister in the Order"; smith openly calls him crazy.
- **PQNine and Hairy visit Carl at the Talking Troll:** late-50s, thinning hair, paint-stained, drawing on peeled-off handbills in the corner. Paints PQNine for **4 cp + 3 mugs of ale + 1 extra ale "to make you handsome."** The result is a stickman. PQNine pretends to tear up. PQNine offers to show Carl his Etch-a-Sketch (Carl refuses to touch it, later tries to charge PQNine to even "say" he likes it). PQNine asks Carl to sign the portrait — Carl can't read or write.
- Back at Northfield, Baron pitches **commissioning a Carl portrait of Sister Halda Thorne to post around Tribor's town square**. Hairy escalates to the slogan "Sister Halda Thorne stinks," which spirals into a long "doesn't smell good" vs "doesn't smell bad" semantics argument with PQNine. Plan parked for the morning.
- Full long rest at Northfield.
