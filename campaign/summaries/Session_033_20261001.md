# Session 33 — Backwater Shopping in Tribor

**Date played:** 2026-10-01  
**Players present:** Fic Torastro (Jake), Tordrug Slaatewalker (Phil), PQNine Ten (Steve), Hairy Styles (sameaslasttime), Baron Halfhammer (Jeff)  
**Source transcript:** `transcripts/Session_033_20261001.docx`  
**Status:** draft

## What happened
- DM delivers a long recap stretching back to Rebel's End, through Luskan, the harpy barge trip, Little Lockford, the Wishkey Gear bringing Shadow Strider back, PQNine's crown-of-madness trap, dumping the gear in the lava, and the Underdark ambush that ended with Sister Halda Thorne unconscious and the party reading her Westbridge/Idrissa Caramorte orders.
- Party argues over whether they "freed" the Little Lockford cannibals Slonk and Uzik; agree they opened the doors but didn't free them. Agree Lord **Hallebrand** (Hairy's target) comes after Shadow Strider is trained.
- Long rest in the wilderness, then trek back to Miller's Reach to wait for the barge. Hairy practices with the silk rope and grappling hook; Fic cants Shadow Strider; PQNine finishes his last shot of whiskey.
- **Jordan Hale** (one-eyed cattle-barge captain) returns, now hauling cattle to Underdark buyers. Hairy pays him 1 gp for passage (vs. 5 sp last time) and later tips him 2 gp in Tribor; Jordan vows to tell everyone about "Harry the Bugbear."
- **River encounter — the bottle:** an old peasant woman on a skiff asks the party to retrieve a clear corked bottle from the reeds. Tordrug Mage Hands it aboard; the note inside reads *"I know what you did."* Hairy Mage Hand-shoves the old woman into the river. **Tordrug keeps the bottle and note.**
- **River encounter — the piano:** a half-submerged white piano drifts downstream, keys plinking on the rocks. A woman on one bank claims it's hers; a man on the other says she pushed it in. Hairy grapple-hooks it, can't lift without tipping the barge, drops it; Fic Fire Bolts the exposed half and sets it on fire. They move on.
- Arrive in **Tribor** at midday.
- **Full Hammer's Forge:** Hairy sells Halda's chainmail — insignia removed — for 20 gp, then renegotiates to **10 gp + a chain-link necklace made from the removed Sisters of the Vale insignia pieces** (Hairy keeps the insignia as a trophy worked into the necklace). Hairy keeps the plate mail and Halda's longsword. Hairy sells a plain shortsword for 4 gp. Tordrug sells a Lord's Alliance longsword for 6 gp and splint armor for 95 gp. Full Hammer warns repeatedly that the Sisters of the Vale are terrifying zealots.
- Fic asks about training **Shadow Strider**; Full Hammer says they'll need a bigger city — Tribor's too small.
- **The Lion's Share** (formerly a local supply shop, newly franchised by the **Lion Shield Coster** of **Yartar**): co-owners **Alestra Algar** and **Narth Tezran**; Narth tries to upsell. Common gear plus four healing potions at 50 gp each. PQNine asks after potions of giant strength (none), "water from a river that flows backwards" (sold out), and the local post office (Narth points them to **Tribor Travelers**).
- Session ends with the party leaving the Lion's Share to find breakfast burritos.

Tribor, having pawned the non-insignia Lord's Alliance gear and most of Halda's non-plate kit, walking out of the Lion's Share hungry. Westbridge is still the next real destination, after a bigger-city stop for horse training.
