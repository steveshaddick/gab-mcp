## Patrons & employers

**Dr. Cassidy "Cassie" Donnell** 🟢 — Human archaeologist with an occult background; Tordrug's old schoolmate (they studied the occult together). Found the Merckmire "stone" and realized it was an egg; fired from the Varkan Bluff Museum after trying to steal it herself. Hired the party for the museum heist [S1]. Mugged by red-robed cultists who took the egg [S4]; paid the party with her last possession, the Eye Stone of Subtle Sight [S4]. Holds Tordrug's Merckmire tome [S5]. Brought Vasil to the Hollow Cask [S7]. Helped fight the hatchling with her ritual knowledge [S12]. Geeky and floundering socially but genuinely brilliant.

**Verity Kai** 🟢 — Red-skinned tiefling gambler. Hates liars (outside the card table). **Revealed as Varen Axebreaker's emissary in Waterdeep** [S15]. Hired the party to rob the Afterlife Casino: her 5000 gp back from former partner Quentin Togglepocket, plus the Erinyes statuette [S3]. Pay 150 gp each + a Bag of Holding. Meets at the Brine Widow. Being asked about by Investigator Vell [S8]. Sold the party a password-protected Bag of Holding for 350 gp [S14].

**Vasil Talistrome** 🟢 — Skinny academic in wire glasses; self-described master of Merckmire lore, infernal realms and demons [S7]. Ran the binding ritual that failed [S8]. Hired the party to recover the Celestial Codex from his ex-student Marcos Delphi: 250 gp each, +50 gp each if Marcos is brought back **alive** [S8]. A previous party he hired died at the mansion. **Relationship broke down [S12]:** he refused to pay after the egg hatched and threatened to report "the Getaround Boys" to the bailiff as thieves. The party menaced him over the Codex. 🔴?

## Allies & companions

**Sister Halda Thorne** 🔴 — Sister of the Vale. Statuesque warrior-nun, longsword, casts Shield of Faith. Arrived at the Hollow Cask demanding the stone: "I came to end something before it begins" [S7]. Fought the party, badly lost, knelt and **yielded** because "our goals are aligned" [S7]. Now travelling and fighting alongside the party [S8–S9]. Did not appreciate Tordrug wearing a cultist robe as a joke. Wanted Marcos destroyed; compromise: she spared him and carried the Celestial Codex [S11]. **Turned on the party [S12]:** she put a sword to Hairy's neck while defending Vasil, and Hairy magic-missiled her. In the missing S13 the party beat her, stripped her armour, shield and sword, and left her bound on the road. Now the Sisters of the Vale have posters up calling the party blasphemers and urging the faithful to capture them [S15]. **Major enemy.** **Ambushed the party in the Underdark [S32]** with crossbow trackers, now sworn to stop them from stealing cursed artifacts and seeking revenge on Hairy: "I mistook you for fools, now I know what you are." Her trackers were killed, and she was **left unconscious and stripped a second time** (plate armor this time). She carried a notebook on cursed objects and sealed orders about **Constantori's portrait**. Her order watches the **Cognoscenti Esoterica**.

**Marlo Drift** 🟢 — Casino piano player; her **Soul Keys** piano's music creates magical illusions/distractions (faked a kitchen fire) [S6–S7]. Loyal to Fontaine. Her price: remove Fontaine's name from the Ledger. **She stole the Ledger from PQNine** and left a black velvet pouch with orphan token #4397 and a note: "You chose judgment. I chose mercy. One of us had to. — M." [S11]. City Hall records show the token is **her own**: left at the South Ward gate aged ~2 and raised in the **Southgate Foundling Home** as "Marlow" [S14]. Admits her own name may be in it and warned PQNine the book "whispers sweeter to the ones who think they're in charge" [S7].

**Fontaine La Rue** 🟡 — Rumpled, greasy, alcoholic card cheat with an "FLR" monogram (DM modeled him on John Candy's Johnny LaRue). Lived in the casino cells since it opened, trading secrets for drinks [S5]. Engineered the vault plan [S5–S6]. Deeply in the Ledger; now deteriorating, shivering, terrified of mirrors [S7]. Party senses he's "an awful man."

**Tobin** 🟢 — Barkeep of the Hollow Cask. Pours the Emberfall Reserve for friends in a pickle [S8]. Mistook the party for "the red robe freaks from down the road" [S7].

**Fiona** 🟢 — Young halfling server at the Hollow Cask; loves hosting; "give me a wink if you're planning something stupid" [S7].

**"Chefy Chef"** 🟢 — Hollow Cask cook; fetches the Emberfall Reserve from the basement [S8].

**Oswald** 🟢 — Bartender at the Brine Widow; runs a tight ship, tolerant of non-humans [S3].

## The Axebreaker job (added S15)

**Varen Axebreaker** 🟢 — Dwarf noble, late 50s–60s, head of a clan thousands of years old; runs a family business (his brother-in-law sits at his table). Verity is his emissary. Hired the party to get the vault key from Prisoner 13; pays 2% of the vault treasure plus 3 magic items [S15, S20]. His agents are going to Gauntlgrym to open the vault. Connected the party to the deep-gnome job [S20].

**Bethra** 🟢 — Varen's dwarf agent in Luskan; supplied the guard uniforms [S15–S16].

**Prisoner 13 ("Quarter Glintstone," sp?)** 🟡 — Former trusted Axebreaker agent who betrayed the clan and stole its fortune. **The tattoo on her right hand is the vault key**; she let the party copy it [S19]. Does as she pleases inside and seems not to want to leave. Stole the Warden's Ledger. "The trickiest case in 20 years."

**The Warden** 🟡 — Lords' Alliance mage, 20 years in corrections (name heard as "Martha…," sp?). Carries the spirit of her dwarf friend **Vlax**, who takes over at times, drinks whiskey and tells war stories. Freeing him would mean a trip to the Brawn Anvil Crypts in Gauntlgrym, which she won't make [S17–S19]. Kind to Hairy; apologized for Hallebrand's atrocities and for the bugbear round-ups [S18]. Doesn't know the party robbed her office.

**Vlax** 🟡 — Dwarf soldier's spirit living in the Warden [S17–S18].

**Vessa Orin** 🟢 — Captain of the Sable Dancer; has a private history with Hairy [S16].

**Borum Feldhand** 🟢 — Shield dwarf, late 180s, iron left leg; runs the Hammer and Hitch in Mirabar. Hides cargo and fakes records for a price [S21].

## Antagonists

**Quentin Togglepocket** 🔴 — Gnome owner of the Afterlife Casino, founded with the gold he stole from Verity [S3, S6]. Flashy, routine-bound, self-obsessed. Has a **pact with the archdevil Mammon** [S7]. Robbed and publicly humiliated by the party [S6–S7].

**Marcos Delphi** 🟢 — Rich heir turned cultist; Vasil's former student. Stole the Celestial Codex; trying to bring **Krokilmar** into the world from his family mansion. Zala calls him Krokilmar's slave [S9]. Last seen mid-ritual over a tentacle-twisted knight's corpse [S10]. **Freed from Krokilmar's influence** when the fragment was destroyed [S11]. Now friendly, a bit "bro-y," free with the wine. Studied the Far Realm at a conjuration school in his late teens and can pronounce Krokilmar's true name. Travelling with the party [S11–S12]; current whereabouts ⚠️.

**Red-robed cultists ("Birthing Moon")** 🔴 — Arcane cult trying to hatch the egg. Wear red robes, leather armor, silver circular symbol talismans. Mugged Donnell [S4]; ritual at the Old Church Road church (slain) [S4]; one tried to intercept Nindle [S8]; four more at the Krokilmar idol in the Delphi basement (slain) [S10].

**Alda Arkin** 🟡 — Elf curator of the Varkan Bluff Museum; emeritus university professor (anthropology), the museum's link to the university. Cutting and vain; charmed by Hairy's flattery [S2]. Clutches a small bag suspected to hold the arcane-lock bypass [S2]. Museum rumored to have cooked books [S2].
