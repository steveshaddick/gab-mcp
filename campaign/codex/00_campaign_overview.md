## The story so far

**Sessions 1–10.**
A disgraced archaeologist, Dr. Cassidy Donnell, hires the crew to steal a "stone" from the Varkan Bluff Museum. The stone is really an egg from the cursed Merckmire, and it is due to hatch at midnight. They pull off the gala heist, then have to rescue the egg again from a red-robed cult at a ruined church. Next comes a job for Verity Kai: they rob the Afterlife Casino of gnome crime boss Quentin Togglepocket, who has a pact with the archdevil Mammon. They leave with 6000 gp and a sinister Ledger of Uncollected Debts. At the Hollow Cask inn, occult scholar Vasil Talistrome tries to bind the egg. PQNine sacrifices a memory for the ritual, but it fails and the Celestial Stone cracks. Along the way, Sister Halda Thorne fights the party, then joins them. Vasil then sends the party to recover the Celestial Codex from his ex-student Marcos Delphi, who is trying to summon the entity **Krokilmar** from his warping family mansion. Session 10 ends with the party facing Marcos mid-ritual in the caverns beneath the house.

**Sessions 11–20.** Beneath the mansion, the party destroyed a fragment of Krokilmar and freed Marcos, who turns out to be a likeable bro. Sister Halda took custody of the Codex. Marlo Drift stole the Ledger from PQNine and left behind her own orphan token. Racing back to the Hollow Cask, the egg hatched inside the Bag of Holding and scattered their treasure across a country road. The party killed the Merckmire Hatchling. Then everything went sideways. Vasil refused to pay, Halda drew on Hairy, and in the unrecorded Session 13 the party beat and stripped Halda. A fire they set in the South Ward killed 600. Wanted posters went up for them, and the Sisters of the Vale declared them blasphemers. Dwarf noble Varen Axebreaker, Verity's real employer, got them out of town. He hired them to infiltrate a Lords' Alliance sea prison as guards and get his clan's vault key from Prisoner 13. The key turned out to be a tattoo on her hand. Along the way Hairy learned the man behind his people's slaughter is now Duke Hallebrand. The party was paid in magic items (and 2% of the vault to come) in Luskan, and they're being pointed toward a deep-gnome town whose machines have gone berserk.

**Sessions 21–32.** Varen's lead took the party through Mirabar, where they hauled silver ingots to Long Saddle and survived a bandit ambush. They crossed the cattle town of Tribor and took the river to **Little Lockford**, a deep-gnome town built on islands over a lake of fire. Its automatons had turned on the townsfolk, and the gnomes hired the party to shut them down. Inside, the party rerouted the bridges and looted the hospital. At the jail they freed prisoners who turned out to be child-eating murderers. In the Smolderton foundry district they found the mad inventor **Fizzlewidget Tinklebottom**, half gnome and half construct, and the **Wishkey Gear**, a machine that bends reality by breaking something else. Fizzlewidget was **PQNine's erased old partner**. The gear nearly claimed PQNine and tempted Hairy with his dead mother's voice before they threw it into the lava. The town paid them grudgingly and then stoned them out, after Tordrug mocked a grieving father. In the Underdark, **Sister Halda** ambushed them again and lost again; they stripped her a second time. Her sealed orders point to **Constantori's portrait** near **Westbridge**, and the Getaround Boys have decided to steal it.

**Sessions 33–35.** Back out of the Underdark, the boys bargelled downriver to Tribor to kit up — selling off most of Halda's gear at Full Hammer's Forge, keeping her Sisters-of-the-Vale insignia as a chain-link necklace, fishing a corked "I know what you did" note out of the river, and setting Fire Bolt to a drifting white piano. In Tribor they drank at the upscale **Northfield / North Shield House** inn, got a stickman portrait from the town drunk Carl, and learned from the locals that **Haravin** at the Harvest Inn in Westbridge was a "dealer in fine art" — resolving the "Haravin?" circled on Halda's map. They rode south to Westbridge, where the halfling innkeeper and bawdy-art fence **Haravin Dar Dragon** fed them burritos and introduced them to **Idrissa Caramorte** of the Cognoscenti Esoterica. In an abandoned barn south of town, Idrissa offered them **2,000 gp** to steal back **Constantori's Portrait** — a sentient painting commissioned by the late Dayani Gristhorne to eavesdrop on its targets — from the thieves' guild called the **Agile Hand**, who had taken it from a Cognoscenti vault and killed three guards doing it. Tordrug Charmed a captive Agile Hand junior named **Grink "Grinky" Brithwort** into drawing a floor-plan of the guild's four-story headquarters and ratting out the lieutenant **Elix the Saint**'s evening habits. The boys started a brawl in the Tribor Travelers caravan yard on the way out (now wanted in Tribor) and tailed Elix from the **Tipsy Tankard** on a dark country road, ran him down with twin Shocking Grasps and a rapier sneak attack, charmed him for intel ("there is no vault, Dusk's office is where the good stuff is"), and then **Tordrug killed him** when the charm wore off. They buried Elix with PQNine's new pewter mug, disguised Hairy as Elix and Tordrug as Grinky, invisibled PQNine and Baron, cloaked Fic, and walked right into the Agile Hand HQ. Inside they looted a leather-bound guild ledger from the quartermaster office, slipped past two sleeping guild members, and found **Guildmaster Dusk** (female, confirmed) asleep in her round tower bedroom with a lover named "Dawn" ⚠️. Fic and Baron climbed an unmapped up-staircase to the Tower Peak and found **Constantori's Portrait** itself — gold-framed, eyes tracking them. The portrait spoke, was not flattered, and **started screaming for help**. The session cut on initiative being rolled with Dusk awake and guards closing on the bedroom.

**Session 36.** In the Tower Peak alcove, Hairy ripped **Constantori's Portrait** off the wall and triggered a hidden **poison-gas trap** (Baron ate the full 33 damage), stuffed the painting into the password-locked Bag of Holding, and the party dropped **Guildmaster Dusk** — Dusk crit Hairy unconscious with her short swords before Baron finished her off at range with Hunter's Mark. Her scantily-clad noble lover smoked a pipe through the whole fight, shrugged off Tordrug's Charm Person, and vanished before the party left. They disguised Fic as Elix, slipped out past the hallway guard in a red Agile Hand cloak, and bedded down in the forest. On second watch, PQNine quietly took the portrait out of the Bag of Holding and **attuned to Constantori as a magic item** — PQNine now has a one-way telepathic link to the painting, a 683-year-old self-styled "most beautiful man in the world" with true sight and the ability to guard a room. By morning Constantori had insulted everyone and spilled a load of secrets: **Idrissa Caramorte wants to question him off the books** about witnessing someone's disappearance, once commissioned a different screaming portrait rumored to hold a man's soul, and is **secretly funding an orphanage with Cognoscenti / guild money**. He also named more Agile Hand loot the party didn't grab (**two spyglasses worth 1,000 gp each** in Elix's office, and a **gold sun-shaped death mask** on a coffin priest), and confirmed **Elix was called "the Saint" because he was the only guild thief who didn't skim**. The session cut as Fic asked Constantori about Lord Hallebrand and the painting confirmed he was "in Hallebrand's presence when he was a young soldier" — the DM paused to look up his own Hallebrand notes.
