# Locations

> Living file. Updated through **Session 32**.

## Waterdeep & surroundings

- **Waterdeep** — The big city; busy streets where trails go cold [S4].
- **Firkin and Egg** — Tavern where the crew first met Donnell; the party's early base [S1, S3].
- **Tavern across the street** — Half-orc bartender with a nose ring; where Donnell recovered after the mugging [S4–S5].
- **Brine Widow** — Well-appointed tavern; Verity Kai's back-room meetings; bartender Oswald [S3].
- **Varkan Bluff Museum of Natural History** — 30-ft ceilings, stone walls. Rooms: grand entrance, ancient plant exhibit, prehistoric predators (a preserved Allosaurus), privies, attic with a skylight, Unearthed Café, creatures-of-the-past wing, archaeology display, records room, and the curator's office (east wing, 1st floor). The Gem/Stone Wing has an arcane-locked pedestal and a 10-ft vent from near the privies. Basement warehouse with a loading dock. Secret door known to a rookie guard [S1–S3].
- **Old Church Road church** — Derelict, desecrated church just outside Waterdeep, with low stone fences, hawthorn and cedar. It has a caretaker's hut, a sanctuary with a shattered obsidian altar, and a food cellar. Cult ritual site; cultists slain [S4–S5].
- **South Ward** — Poor district. **A third of it burned in the party's fire, with 600 dead** [S15]. The Watch command tent is on the **Way of the Dragon**. **Tamora's Blessing** tavern is here.
- **Southgate Foundling Home** — Just inside the South Ward gate, where abandoned children are left. Marlo Drift was raised here [S14].
- **City Hall** — Ward-of-the-city records and property lookups [S14].
- **The Font of Knowledge** — Appointment-only stone bookshop beside the temple in the **Castle Ward**, run by Barlow Lundegger for Bilaro Quist [S14].
- **Bilaro Quist's manor** — North Ward [S14].
- **"The Other Place"** — Pub across from the Brine Widow [S12].
- **Waterdeep's burrito stalls** — Home of the Owlbear Hug and the Dawn Patrol. The Brine Widow serves breakfast burritos too (the "Crit Roll" special) [S14–S15].
- **Briar Thistle Crossing** — Road south of which a cultist tried to intercept Nindle [S8].

## The Hollow Cask

Inn at a crossroads, built of river stone and timber, with a thatched roof, ivy, and cottages out back. Staff are Tobin, Fiona and Chefy Chef, and the house special is the Emberfall Reserve. The ritual clearing is behind the cottages at the edge of the wood [S7–S8]. The cult is "down the road."

## The Afterlife Casino

Built inside a mountain, reached by carriage lot, then Charon's boat down the "River Styx" into a cave, with lava beneath. It has a Nine Hells theme and all-tiefling staff (female floor staff, male security). House rules sign; green-trimmed doors are for employees only (passcards). Scrying mirrors 12 ft up in every section. Areas: copper slots, cashier stations, a rat-racing track (named for Mammon), a circus, a waterfall overlook, the Three-Dragon Ante ring (black basalt, 7 tables), Life and Death dice, the **Nessus** restaurant, spa and baths, laundry, a break room, holding cells, a security room with a mirror wall, Quentin's office, the clerk's office, and the vault behind fire-breathing devil-face doors [S3, S5–S6].

## The Delphi Mansion

Decaying family estate with alabaster walls and three floors joined by a great spiral staircase. **The house warps**: doors turn into biting mouths. A mansion clock tolls the countdown [S8–S10].
- **3rd floor:** collapsed roof (Zala the nothic), stirge room, Esquire's ritual circle, star-map room (elf ghost), servants' notes [S9].
- **2nd floor:** portrait gallery with alabaster busts, office, bedroom, hidden closet with the trapped lockbox [S10].
- **1st floor / basement:** ruined kitchen; chapel of the Krokilmar idol (smashed); storage room with the previous party's bodies; cellar with casks leading to a spiral stair [S10].
- **Caverns below:** two circular caves lit purple by four chanting-synced crystals. Marcos's ritual is here [S10].
- **Elra Lionheart's camp:** due south of the mansion [S8].

## The Sword Coast North (added S15–S21)

- **Thornhold** — Harbor and logistics town where Hairy dug up the Hallebrand story [S16].
- **Luskan** — Rough port. The haberdasher Maurice is here, and so are **the Keelhauler's Mercy** (a packed dock pub where you pay into a bowl of coins, teeth and rings) and **the Last Warm Light** (a safe inn by the inland gate, private rooms 2 gp/night) [S16, S20]. It also has the **Northgate Teamsters yard** for horses [S21].
- **The Lords' Alliance prison ("the Panopticon")** — A sea fortress about 3 days' sail north of Luskan, cold and windy, with a central tower and spiral stair, barracks, kitchens, a yard and a hexagonal cell block. The Warden's office is on the 3rd floor and magically sealed. There's a suspicion/alert system: horns sound and the continual flames turn red [S16–S19].
- **Gauntlgrym** — Dwarven fortress under **Mount Hotenow** near Neverwinter. It holds the **Axebreaker vault** and the **Brawn Anvil Crypts**, where Vlax's kin rest [S15, S17, S19].
- **Mirabar** — Fortified dwarven trade city full of caravans. Home of the **Hammer and Hitch** inn and caravan yard (Borum Feldhand) [S21].
- **Cormier College ruins** — Orc-infested; a long-sought tome lies beneath [S14].
- **Hallebrand Province** — The former "Wilds" to the north [S16].

## The road to Little Lockford (added S21–S32)

- **Brindlewick and Sons Pressure and Motion Works** — Mirabar gnome-engineering shop [S22].
- **Long Saddle** — Hamlet whose town hall received the silver [S22].
- **Tribor** — Frontier cattle town full of deep gnomes; no magic shop (try the caravans). **The Talking Troll** pub serves beef and onion pie [S23].
- **Timber Wharf → Miller's Reach** — The canal and river route toward Little Lockford; river wedding; something in the water charms people [S24].
- **Westbridge** — Farming village south of Tribor where the Long Road meets the Stone Trail. Circled on Halda's map with "Haravin?" [S32]. **Next destination?**

## Little Lockford (deep-gnome town)

Islands **suspended over a lake of fire**, linked by retractable bridges and a mechanical ore **bucket chain**. Abandoned after the automatons turned; the townsfolk live outside the sealed gates (knock 7 times to get back out) [S25].
- **Halfway Aisle** — Domed temple of Callarduran Smoothhands [S26].
- **Turbine Heights** — Power station with a 30-ft crackling tower; its **control console** has switches for every bridge and district, the bucket chain, and a station shutdown. Also the infirmary [S25–S26].
- **Cave Mouth District** — Where the monsters are; the party cut it off by raising its bridge [S26–S28].
- **Smolderton** — Grimy foundry district that reeks of burning metal. It holds **Fizzlewidget's workshop** (Crown of Madness circles, the safe with the onyx box, the forge) and the **fail-safe device** above the lava. The Wishkey Gear went into the lava here [S27–S31].
- **The Mother Lode Tavern** — Clockwork defenders and observers [S30].
- **Hospital and jail** — Looted; the jail's cannibal prisoners were freed [S27–S28].
- **The Underdark approach** — Where Halda's ambush happened [S32].

## Far places

- **The Merckmire** — Remote, cursed, sunken swamp; source of the egg and of children's scare stories. Its muck kept the entity dormant [S1–S2, S7]. Hairy saw it in a vision [S6–S7].
